# Two-to-three-minute presentation

Prepare the services, funded channels and before snapshot ahead of time. Keep
Alice and Bob tabs visible. Use the current domain and policy. The presenter
executes economic actions manually; never prepare a hidden replacement payment.

* **0:00–0:25:** Show distinct wallet names and Bob's copyable recipient address.
  “The agent discovers the recipient and requests an invoice. It cannot approve
  a payment itself.” Avoid showing terminal credentials.
* **0:25–1:05:** Alice asks `Pay bob@<domain> 5 R402USD.` Show the application
  approval sheet with exact recipient, amount and asset. Approve once and wait
  for authoritative settlement; show Bob's corresponding received entry.
* **1:05–1:30:** Show native BTC capability with `Pay bob@<domain> 10 sats.`
  Point out the separate BTC approval and sats denomination. Approve only if this
  is the scheduled manual acceptance; otherwise show recorded settled evidence.
* **1:30–2:10:** Ask `Get the premium report.` Show the 3-sat policy decision and
  verified resource result. Repeat it to show the same payment hash and reuse.
  Explain whether the initial request was a fresh purchase or recovery of an
  existing purchase. If old credentials have expired, show the historical capture
  and explain the current blocked result; never label that live resource success.
* **2:10–2:40:** Ask for the configured extended report. Show 50-sat approval,
  choose Cancel and show no payment. “Small machine purchases can execute within
  policy; larger purchases stop for the human.”

End on the activity list and the two wallets. Settlement timing may exceed this
schedule: wait or use clearly labeled historical evidence, not a fabricated
success. Keep raw logs, invoices and infrastructure setup out of the presentation.
